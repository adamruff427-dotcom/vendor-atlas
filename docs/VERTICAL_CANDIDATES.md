## 6 October 2026: fire damper testing

Decision: hold. No route, supplier listing or price calculator was added to the public product. This is an evidence decision, not a technical or permission blocker. Assess a different category on the next daily run; revisit this one only when new applicable pricing evidence is available.

### Duty and scope evidence

The [Home Office guide for persons with fire-safety duties](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/people-with-duties-under-fire-safety-laws/a-guide-for-persons-with-duties-under-fire-safety-legislation-accessible), section 18.4, includes fire dampers among systems needing inspection or testing and servicing where provided. The guide applies to England. The underlying [Fire Safety Order article 17](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2005/1541/article/17) maintenance duty is the England/Wales starting point; Scotland and Northern Ireland need separate legal review. The candidate would cover existing assets, not decide whether a building needs new dampers.

[BSI describes BS 9999 as recommendations and guidance](https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/products/fire-safety-in-the-design-management-and-use-of-buildings-code-of-practice), not a statute. Provider statements equating an annual BS 9999 interval with legislation are not admitted as regulatory evidence. The exact interval must follow the applicable standard, manufacturer and premises regime. [NHS England HTM 03-01](https://www.england.nhs.uk/publication/specialised-ventilation-for-healthcare-buildings/) is healthcare guidance; its application outside acute care requires a facility risk assessment. Healthcare work would require bespoke scope rather than a generic commercial estimate.

### Buyer questionnaire and comparable quote proposal

A provisional brief could record jurisdiction, responsible person, premises use, asset register, damper type, known mechanical versus motorised units, count by site, previous test findings, existing access panels, access height, ventilation shutdown window, known faults and required report date. Unknown assets would route to a locating survey first. No installed assets would route to provision review, not a testing quote.

The bounded purchase would be inspection and function testing of listed, safely accessible assets, with asset-level results and restoration recorded. Locating surveys, new access openings, asbestos investigation, specialist access equipment, repairs, replacement and smoke-control cause-and-effect work would be separate. A competent contractor must agree the method, safe isolation and restoration. The questionnaire would not choose these controls or establish compliance.
